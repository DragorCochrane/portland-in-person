# Audit — November 2026
**Date checked:** 2026-11-05 (all rows) · **Scope:** all 67 listings in `index.html`, the dead-ends list, and new fall/winter 2026-27 additions.
## Method and limits
Nine research agents ran in parallel: seven re-checked existing listings in batches of about ten, and two looked for new listings. The session's network policy blocked direct page loads (WebFetch and curl failed for every organization's domain), so **every finding rests on WebSearch results that quote or index the organizations' own pages, plus Meetup, ticketing and directory listings**. Nothing was checked word for word on a live page. Source URLs below are the pages those results pointed to. Listings marked *unverifiable* stay in the directory with a △ caveat. None were removed, because no listing turned up clear evidence of closure.
